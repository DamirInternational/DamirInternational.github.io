Compliance & Transaction Process

A bank-compliant sequence, from first contact to delivery

Every transaction follows the same documented sequence. No step is skipped, and no commitment is made before the previous one is complete.

  1. 01

    NDA & KYC

    Mutual NDA and full Know-Your-Customer review of each counterparty, with screening against OFAC, EU and UN sanctions lists.

  2. 02

    Term Sheet

    Commercial terms agreed in writing: product, volume, Incoterm, delivery window, price mechanism and payment structure.

  3. 03

    Sale & Purchase Agreement

    Execution of a full SPA reflecting the agreed term sheet, with complete product specifications and loading windows.

  4. 04

    Banking Instruments

    Payment secured by a dual instrument: Standby Letter of Credit (MT760, ISP98) and Letter of Credit at sight (MT700, UCP 600).

  5. 05

    Inspection & Delivery

    Independent inspection at the load port (SGS or equivalent), Certificate of Analysis and Certificate of Origin, CIF delivery to Asia.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does Damir International verify its counterparties?

Every counterparty goes through a mutual NDA and a full KYC review, including screening against OFAC, EU and UN sanctions lists, before any commercial terms are discussed.

Which payment instruments do you accept?

Payment is secured by a Standby Letter of Credit (MT760, ISP98) together with a Letter of Credit at sight (MT700, UCP 600), issued by the buyer's bank.

Where does the crude oil come from?

Supply origins for the 2026–2027 programme are the United Arab Emirates, Oman and Kuwait. All barrels are non-sanctioned and supplied with full compliance documentation.

Which delivery terms and destinations are available?

Deliveries are made on a CIF basis to Asia, with destination flexibility between Singapore, South China, Korea and Japan.

How is product quality guaranteed?

Each cargo is inspected independently at the load port by SGS or an equivalent inspector, and delivered with a Certificate of Analysis and a Certificate of Origin.
